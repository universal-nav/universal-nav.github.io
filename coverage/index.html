Where the corpus was collected

← back to the project page

Every track below is a segment the rig travelled on foot. The corpus also contains transit legs — subway rides, lifts, escalators — and those are deliberately absent: they are displacement, but they are not collected sidewalk. They are identified by a vision–language model run over pairs of frames two seconds apart, which is what lets a stationary carriage interior be told apart from a moving one. Segments where the lens was covered are dropped for the same reason.

Tracks are median-smoothed before drawing. The receiver on this rig reports σ 30–70 m, so raw fix-to-fix polylines zig-zag across the street and sum to more than twice the true path length; positions here are good enough to say which block, and not much finer. Distances on this page are not measured from GPS — they come from wheel-free visual odometry whose metric scale is pinned by the rig's depth sensor, with device odometry preferred wherever it is locally coherent. Summing GPS instead would claim 81 km against a true 34 km.
